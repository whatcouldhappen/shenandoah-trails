Old Rag Mountain Loop

A wooded climb, a hands-on granite scramble and a summit with views in every direction. From Skyland, plan on driving to the Old Rag parking area at the park boundary.

Distance
9.4 miles
Elevation gain
2,348 feet
Hiking time
7½ hours
NPS rating
Very strenuous

Up the Ridge Trail

From the parking area, follow the blue blazes uphill through the woods. On the ridge the walking rhythm breaks: you thread between granite blocks and use your hands to pull up onto ledges. An early overlook feels like the top, but the summit is farther along.

Prefer to skip the scramble? The Berry Hollow out-and-back reaches the same summit and avoids the Ridge Trail scramble. The summit itself is still open rock.

Over the summit and down through the hollow

At the summit, the trees give way to open granite. The loop still has a long descent ahead: follow the Saddle Trail past Byrds Nest and Old Rag shelters, then turn onto Weakley Hollow Fire Road. The Ridge Access Trail leads back toward the parking area.

Planning a May hike

The park currently requires an advance Old Rag day-use ticket from March 1 through November 30, as well as park admission. Check the park’s ticket information before the trip.
