Pass Mountain Loop

Head north from Thornton Gap for a woodland hike to Pass Mountain Hut. The shelter makes a natural lunch stop, and the open woods give you room to look for spring wildflowers and listen for birds along the Appalachian Trail.

3.4 miles

670 feet

2½ hours

Moderate

What to expect on the hike

Hikers enjoyed the views and quieter stretches, and some described the paths as easy enough for a family outing. The hut gives you a place to stop for lunch.

The park rates the Panorama route moderate, with 670 feet of climbing. Reviewers mention bugs and suggest an early start for a quieter walk.

Based on a reader-supplied summary of AllTrails hiker reviews.

From Skyland to Panorama

Drive north on Skyline Drive to the Panorama parking area at mile 31.6, about ten road miles from Skyland’s north entrance. This is also the starting point for Marys Rock’s north approach. At the Appalachian Trail junction the two hikes split: right for Pass Mountain, left for Marys Rock.

Through the woods to the hut

Take the connector at the back of the parking area and turn north on the Appalachian Trail. The route crosses both US 211 and Skyline Drive. Farther along, turn right onto the fire road and follow it to Pass Mountain Hut.

Leave the hut on the blue-blazed Pass Mountain Trail to close the loop. When you meet the Appalachian Trail again, head south and retrace the approach to Panorama. For a May hike, the hut and the spring woods are the main draws; this park itinerary does not require a summit scramble.
