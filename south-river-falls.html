South River Falls and Fire Road Loop

The trail descends through woodland to a stone-walled overlook facing the 83-foot South River Falls. Follow the stream through the woods, then make the climb back on the fire road and the Appalachian Trail.

Loop distance
3.3 miles
Elevation gain
910 feet
Hiking time
2 hours 15 minutes
NPS rating
Moderate

What to expect on the hike

The overlook frames the waterfall through the trees. Fall hikers liked the surrounding colors, and some reviewers preferred the closer view from the base.

The base is an extra side trip beyond the overlook circuit described here. Factor in that additional walking before committing to the rocky climb back to the picnic grounds.

Based on a reader-supplied summary of AllTrails hiker reviews.

Getting there from Skyland

Head south on Skyline Drive to the South River Picnic Grounds at milepost 62.7. The trail starts below the restrooms. From Skyland’s dining room, allow about 50 minutes for the 21.6-mile drive. See parking and directions.

Walking the loop

Take the blue-blazed trail across the Appalachian Trail and down to the overlook. To complete the circuit, continue to the trail junction beyond the viewpoint. Turn left onto South River Falls Road and follow it onto South River Fire Road. When you reach the Appalachian Trail, turn left, then right onto the falls trail to return to the picnic grounds.
