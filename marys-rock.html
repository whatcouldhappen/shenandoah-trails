Marys Rock

From Panorama at Thornton Gap, the north approach follows the Appalachian Trail up to an open rock viewpoint over the Shenandoah Valley. Most of the work comes on the way out. On the return, you follow the same trail downhill.

What to expect on the hike

The north approach climbs steadily through the woods before opening onto the summit rocks. Hikers liked the valley views and found the path well maintained.

Most of the 1,210 feet of climbing comes before the viewpoint. Reviewers suggest sturdy footwear for the rocky tread and an early arrival on busy days.

Getting there from Skyland

Drive north on Skyline Drive to the Panorama parking area near mile 31.6, about 10 road miles from Skyland’s north entrance. You’ll pass the Meadow Spring trailhead on the way. Continue to Panorama for the north approach shown on this map.

From Panorama to the summit

At the back of the parking area, take the short connector to the Appalachian Trail. Turn left to hike south. The climb gains roughly 1,200 feet in less than two miles. At the signed trail junction, turn right onto the spur to the viewpoint.

At the top, look west across the Shenandoah Valley toward the long ridge of Massanutten Mountain. To return, follow the spur back to the Appalachian Trail, then head north to the Panorama connector.

If you prefer to avoid cliff edges, the summit viewpoint is the place to make that call. Stay back from the edge of the outcrop and respect the park’s posted closures protecting vegetation on the rock.
