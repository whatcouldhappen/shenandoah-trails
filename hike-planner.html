How is uphill grade estimated?

We divide total elevation gain by half the hike distance in feet, then multiply by 100. This assumes half the route is uphill and treats trail mileage as approximate horizontal distance. Flats, rolling terrain and short steep pitches can change the actual slope. Colors compare the estimated average climb across these trails. Custom hikes without matching trail data appear gray.
