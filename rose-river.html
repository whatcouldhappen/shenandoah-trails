Rose River Falls Loop

From Fishers Gap, the trail drops through the woods to the 67-foot Rose River Falls. The climb comes on the way back, where the path follows a series of smaller cascades before meeting the fire road.

Loop distance
4 miles
Elevation gain
910 feet
Hiking time
4½ hours
NPS rating
Moderate

What to expect on the hike

The smaller cascades along the stream drew almost as much attention as Rose River Falls itself. Several May hikers liked going clockwise from Fishers Gap, reaching the falls before returning along the fire road.

Most reviewers rated the loop moderate. Save some energy for the climb after the falls. May hikers who went after rain described stronger waterfall flow, but also muddy stretches, slick boulders and a trickier stream crossing.

Getting there from Skyland

Drive south on Skyline Drive to Fishers Gap parking at milepost 49.4. Allow about 19 minutes for the 8-mile drive from the lodge. See parking and driving directions.

Walking the loop

Cross Skyline Drive from the north end of the lot. Beyond the fire-road gate, turn left onto the horse trail. After about 0.6 mile, the blue blazes lead downhill toward the falls. Beyond the bridge, expect a steep, rocky climb along the cascades. Turn right at the fire road for the final uphill mile to Skyline Drive.

See both waterfalls

Start at Fishers Gap and follow the Rose River loop past Rose River Falls. When you reach the fire road, turn right across the bridge. Before heading back up the fire road, take the Dark Hollow Falls Trail to the base of the waterfall. Retrace that spur to the fire road, then continue uphill to Fishers Gap.

The side trip adds about ¼ mile round trip, bringing the outing to about 4¼ miles total from the same parking lot. Park Service directions · Big Meadows trail map (PDF)
