Rose River Falls Loop

From Fishers Gap, the trail drops through the woods to the 67-foot Rose River Falls. The climb comes on the way back, where the path follows a series of smaller cascades before meeting the fire road.

Loop distance
4 miles
Elevation gain
910 feet
Hiking time
4½ hours
NPS rating
Moderate

Getting there from Skyland

Drive south on Skyline Drive to Fishers Gap parking at milepost 49.4. Allow about 19 minutes for the 8-mile drive from the lodge. See parking and driving directions.

Walking the loop

Cross Skyline Drive from the north end of the lot. Beyond the fire-road gate, turn left onto the horse trail. After about 0.6 mile, the blue blazes lead downhill toward the falls. Beyond the bridge, expect a steep, rocky climb along the cascades. Turn right at the fire road for the final uphill mile to Skyline Drive.
