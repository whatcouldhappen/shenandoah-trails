Calvary and Chimney Rocks Trail

Chimney Rock comes into view across a gorge. The walk there is part of the appeal: the Riprap Trail winds through switchbacks, passes an overlook, then brings you alongside the formations at Calvary Rocks.

Round trip
3.4 miles
Elevation gain
830 feet
Hiking time
2½ hours
NPS rating
Moderate

Jump to the trail map

Getting there from Skyland

Drive south on Skyline Drive to the Riprap parking area at mile 90. From Skyland’s north entrance at mile 41.7, that’s about 48 road miles each way. Allow for the drive separately from the park’s hiking time.

From Riprap to the gorge

Start north on the Appalachian Trail from the parking area, then turn left onto the Riprap Trail. Follow the switchbacks to the first opening on your right. Farther along, look to your left for Calvary Rocks.

The landmark for the turnaround is a sharp left bend in the trail. Look right across the gorge for the freestanding Chimney Rock. From here, return the way you came. Continuing beyond the viewpoint takes you farther into the Riprap backcountry.

At the viewpoint, you can look across at the formation from the trail. Reaching or climbing the rock across the gorge is outside the park’s described hike. Give the exposed edges a wide berth.
