Dark Hollow Falls Trail

The trail follows a stream downhill through the woods to the waterfall. Save time and energy for the return: the same rocky steps that lead down to the falls make for a sustained climb back to Skyline Drive.

Round trip
1.4 miles
Elevation gain
440 feet
Hiking time
1 hour 15 minutes
NPS rating
Moderate

Getting there from Skyland

Drive south on Skyline Drive to the Dark Hollow Falls parking area at milepost 50.7. From the Skyland dining room, the estimated one-way drive is 9.1 miles, or about 21 minutes. See parking and driving directions.

Want to see both falls from one parking spot? Start at Fishers Gap for the 4¼-mile Rose River loop with a side trip to Dark Hollow Falls.
