Sugarloaf Loop

A circuit through Shenandoah’s North District ties the Piney Branch woods to the Appalachian Trail near Hogback Overlook. Mountain laurel grows along the route; the park puts its white blooms in early summer, so a May visit may be too early to catch them.

Getting there from Skyland

Drive north on Skyline Drive to the Piney River parking area at mile 22. That’s about 20 road miles from Skyland’s north entrance. Start on Piney Branch Upper Trail for the park’s direction around the loop.

Through the woods and back along the ridge

Follow Piney Branch Upper Trail to the Pole Bridge Link junction. Turn left onto the link, then left again onto Sugarloaf Trail. This stretch brings you back toward Skyline Drive above Hogback Overlook.

At the Appalachian Trail, turn left and follow its white blazes back toward the parking area. The Keyser Run area map shows all four named trail segments together, which makes it useful for checking the turns before setting out.
