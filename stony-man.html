Stony Man via the Appalachian Trail

A short woodland climb near Skyland opens onto a rocky overlook facing the Shenandoah Valley and Massanutten Mountain. It’s a good choice for an arrival afternoon with a clear sky.

What to expect on the hike

A short woodland climb opens onto a rocky ledge overlooking the Shenandoah Valley. On a clear day, look across the valley for Massanutten Mountain.

AllTrails rates the route moderate, although 65% of hikers who rated the difficulty called it easy. The summit footing is uneven, and hikers report spotty phone service. Save the map before leaving Skyland.

Based on the AllTrails route description, hiker difficulty poll and cell-coverage reports. View trail on AllTrails

From Skyland to the overlook

Start at the Stony Man parking area by Skyland’s north entrance, at Skyline Drive mile 41.7. The park’s mileage starts at the parking area. If you walk from your room, add the distance to and from the trailhead.

Follow the white blazes of the Appalachian Trail uphill through the woods. At the trail post, pick up the blue blazes of the Stony Man Trail to the viewpoint. On the return, continue around the Stony Man Trail to meet the Appalachian Trail again, then follow it downhill to the parking area.

At the overlook the footing changes from woodland trail to open rock with a drop-off. If heights bother you, pause well back from the rim or turn around where the trees give way to rock.
