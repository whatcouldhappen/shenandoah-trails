White Oak Canyon to Upper Falls Overlook

From Skyline Drive, descend through the woods to a rocky ledge overlooking Upper Whiteoak Falls. The overlook is the turnaround point, 2.3 miles from the trailhead. Save your energy for the steep climb back to the car.

Round trip
4.6 miles
Elevation gain
1,040 feet
Hiking time
3½ hours
NPS rating
Moderate

What to expect on the hike

The canyon is known among hikers for its series of waterfalls and pools. This particular hike turns around at the Upper Falls overlook, so plan on that view as the destination.

The rocky descent puts the steep climb back to Skyline Drive in the second half. Reviewers mention crowded parking and suggest carrying plenty of water and a snack for the return.

Based on a reader-supplied summary of AllTrails hiker reviews.

Getting there from Skyland

Use the Whiteoak Canyon parking area on Skyline Drive at milepost 42.6. From the Skyland dining room, it’s about 1.1 miles by road, an estimated 4-minute drive. See parking and driving directions.

Walking to the overlook

Follow the Whiteoak Canyon Trail past the Old Rag Fire Road and Limberlost Trail junctions. At the upper falls viewpoint, turn back along the same trail. Continuing downhill takes you farther into the canyon toward the other waterfalls.
