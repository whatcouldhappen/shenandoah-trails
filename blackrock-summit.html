Blackrock Summit

A short stretch of the Appalachian Trail leads out of the woods to a slope of broken rock. The trail curves around the boulder field, opening views across the Shenandoah Valley toward Massanutten Mountain.

Loop distance
1 mile
Elevation gain
175 feet
Hiking time
45 minutes
NPS rating
Easiest

Getting there from Skyland

Head south on Skyline Drive to Blackrock parking at milepost 84.4. The estimated one-way drive from the Skyland dining room is 43.1 miles, about 1 hour 34 minutes. See parking and driving directions.

Walking the loop

Follow the Appalachian Trail south from the parking area. At the trail post, bear right toward the boulders and stay on the trail as it wraps around the slope. Turn left at the Blackrock Hut Road and Trayfoot Mountain Trail junction to loop back to the car.
