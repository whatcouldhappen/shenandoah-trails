Compton Peak via the Appalachian Trail

A wooded climb from Compton Gap leads to two short side trails. The western spur opens onto a mountain view. The eastern spur leads to a rock face where ancient lava cooled into columns.

Round trip
2.4 miles
Elevation gain
855 feet
Hiking time
1 hour 45 minutes
NPS rating
Moderate

Getting there from Skyland

Drive north on Skyline Drive to Compton Gap parking at milepost 10.4. From the Skyland dining room, the estimated drive is 31.6 miles, about 69 minutes each way. See parking and directions.

West overlook and east spur

Cross Skyline Drive from the parking area and follow the Appalachian Trail south. At the spur junction, turn right for the western view. Return to the junction and continue straight for the eastern side. The blue-blazed descent below the cliff to see the columns is optional and has rough, rocky footing. Return to the junction and take the Appalachian Trail north to the car.
